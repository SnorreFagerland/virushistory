

File virus.r
This is a self-replicant program I wrote around 2003, it's written in Rebol and only infected other Rebol files. 
The interesting points about this experiment are: 
1. It's written in a single line of code 
2. AFAIK there were no other viruses ever created in Rebol. I even sent it to the author of Rebol, Carl Sessenrath (one of the authors of the AmigaOS), but he never responded. 
An inspiration for this work was the previous works by Pablo Garaizar (teacher at the University of Deusto, back then a PhD student), creating self-replicant programs in bash 
scripting, presented in the Hackmeeting of Bilbao in Sep 2001.

I edited the comments to remove any personal reference.
